Intelligence Pipeline

From signal to response in 7 steps

Every decision KAVACH makes is traceable, explainable and auditable.

01

Collect 16 Telemetry Collectors

Background daemons continuously observe low-level OS events across processes, sockets, DNS, registry, PowerShell and canary tripwires.

02

Normalize Async Event Bus & Deduplication

Raw events pass through an asynchronous in-memory bus. Fields are standardised into canonical schemas, noisy heartbeats are deduplicated and correlation IDs stamped.

03

Detect Deterministic Rules + IsolationForest ML

Each event is evaluated by two engines at once: rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK and an IsolationForest model over 10 behavioural dimensions.

04

Correlate Incident Clustering & Kill-Chain Mapping

Alerts are correlated over time across hosts, accounts and network destinations to build a full incident timeline.

05

Score Hybrid Risk Engine

Rule severity, ML anomaly confidence and threat-intel lookups combine into a transparent, calibrated 0–100 risk score.

06

Respond SOAR Playbook Execution

Controlled actions fire per security policy — high-impact ones need authorisation, support dry-run and log an immutable audit trail.

07

Explain Raksha AI Cybersecurity Copilot

Raksha turns evidence, process trees and actions into clear explanations for everyday users and deep forensic summaries for analysts.

Under the hood

Design choices that make it trustworthy

Nothing in the pipeline is a black box.

01

Two detectors, not one

Rules catch what is known. ML catches what is strange. An event only escalates when the evidence agrees.

02

A score you can read

Risk is 0 to 100 and every point can be traced back to a rule, a model output or a threat-intel match.

03

Humans stay in control

High-impact actions need authorisation, can be rehearsed in dry-run and are fully logged.
